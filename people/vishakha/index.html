3In detail

Her path
TimelineAWS cloud/support engineer → Google Cloud Architect → NVIDIA Solutions Architect. At Google, years 1–2 she was a Kubernetes specialist (DevOps, CI/CD, containers), which gave her an edge. Years 3–4 she upskilled in AI workflows. She started interviewing with NVIDIA "last year" and took 6–8 months to land the role.
What she did with customersCustomers deployed custom models on GKE. She advised on storage placement close to compute, optimizing clusters, placing GPU nodes together, topology-aware scheduling, and observability (Prometheus, Grafana). As an architect she designed, connected components, ran PoCs and gave end-to-end demos, rather than being hands-on.
Two paths in cloud + AI
AI infrastructure (her pick for you)How models are trained and served on Kubernetes (EKS/GKE), serverless or raw instances. Infra knowledge of compute, storage, networking between GPU nodes, and data transfer latency.
Managed ML servicesSageMaker-style: pick a model, customize in notebooks, serve an endpoint. Closer to data science; a big umbrella with lots of buzzwords and competition.
Why infra"We are not data scientists, not AI engineers building LLM apps. We help get models deployed and served." Experienced hires (5–7 yrs) are expected at intermediate–advanced level.
Inference = a black box to optimizeA prompt that takes 5 s is a bad experience. Your job is to get it to 2–3 s through model tuning and job scheduling on Kubernetes.
How she prepared
The course as a gap mapShe took NVIDIA's paid AI Infrastructure & Operations course (didn't take the certificate). It gave her a mind map of what she didn't know. Then, for each topic, the job description + a chatbot to go deeper, and a whiteboard with daily topics (e.g. quantization techniques). By interview time she knew "at least a little" about each.
Storage example she gaveObject, file and block storage behave differently for ML. Models on Kubernetes load weights from remote object storage, so transfer speed decides how fast a model loads and serves. Decide: object storage, hot storage nearby, or a local cache on the VM (cloud caching services exist).
Interview shapeLike AWS: several rounds. For SA: technical rounds, design rounds, deep dives on infrastructure for ML applications. A coding or scripting online assessment is possible. All traditional-app knowledge must translate to ML workloads: training, inference, preprocessing.
Kubernetes securityBeyond IAM: application and infrastructure security, how microservices communicate, RBAC, then OPA Gatekeeper and policy admission controllers.
Advanced KubernetesCRDs and operators are crucial for ML workloads; some companies ask you to write custom operators (requires code). Kubernetes helps in any future role, even data engineering. Certification optional, but CKA is often preferred.
